## 🌟 Featured Projects

### 🩸 Shoryan — Blood Donation AI Platform

An AI-powered blood donation platform designed to help patients find compatible donors more efficiently.

**AI Engineering:**

* 🔎 Hybrid **FAISS + BM25 retrieval**
* 🎯 Cross-Encoder **reranking**
* 🧠 Context engineering & token budgeting
* 🌍 Arabic & English language support
* 🛡️ Grounded generation & answer verification
* 📊 ML-based donor availability prediction
* ⚡ FastAPI backend for AI services

---

### 🤖 Rafiq — Shoryan's Grounded AI Chatbot

Rafiq is the intelligent chatbot of **Shoryan**, providing bilingual assistance through a **retrieval-first architecture**. It is designed to answer users' questions about blood donation and keep responses grounded in trusted knowledge while reducing hallucinations.

**Focus:**
`RAG` • `Information Retrieval` • `LLMs` • `Safety` • `Evaluation`

---

### ✋ Smart Sign — ASL Recognition System

Computer vision system for recognizing American Sign Language gestures using deep learning.

**Technologies:**
`CNN` • `ResNet50` • `TensorFlow` • `OpenCV` • `Streamlit` • `FastAPI`
